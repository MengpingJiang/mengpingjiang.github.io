## Open Positions

We are actively seeking highly motivated and enthusiastic postdoctoral fellows and graduate students to join our team, and we provide a supportive environment and value the continued growth and success of all lab personnel.

### Research Opportunities

Our research centers on **cooperation and social coordination**, with developing interests in **group behavior and collective dynamics** and **social learning and adaptation**. Our long-term program also includes social intelligence across biological and artificial systems, and social dysfunction and intervention.

We welcome applicants with backgrounds in neuroscience, biomedical engineering, computer science, automation, psychology, and medicine. Projects will be matched to the laboratory's current priorities, each applicant's expertise, and available resources.

[Read about our research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }})

### Graduate Students

We welcome students interested in quantitative social behavior, neural circuits, or computational approaches. Curiosity, careful reasoning, and a willingness to learn across disciplines are particularly valuable.

### Postdoctoral Researchers
We particularly welcome expertise in systems or computational neuroscience, including animal behavior, neural recording and circuit manipulation, behavioral modeling, neural data analysis, or multi-agent learning.

### Research Assistants

Relevant skills include animal training and care, behavioral experiments, histology, experimental coordination, programming, and data analysis.

Interested applicants should send:

- CV
- a summary of your current and future research interests
- Contact information for references (three references)
