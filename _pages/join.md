## Join Our Team

The **Laboratory for Social and Computational Neuroscience (LSCN)** at Shanghai Jiao Tong University's School of Biomedical Engineering welcomes inquiries from prospective **postdocs, master's and Ph.D. students, research assistants, and interns**.

We welcome backgrounds in neuroscience, biology, medicine, biomedical engineering, computer science, automation, psychology, and other disciplines. Applicants can contribute through experimental or computational expertise; experience in every technique is not expected.

We value curiosity about neuroscience, initiative, and a thoughtful, constructive approach to research challenges.

[Email Mengping Jiang](mailto:mpjiang2021@163.com)

</div>

## Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome researchers who have completed, or are approaching completion of, a Ph.D. in a relevant field and want to develop an independent research direction within a collaborative laboratory.

Relevant strengths include:

- **Experimental neuroscience:** quantitative animal behavior, neural recording, optogenetics, chemogenetics, or circuit analysis.
- **Computational neuroscience:** behavioral modeling, neural population analysis, dynamical systems, or statistical inference.
- **AI and engineering:** multi-agent learning, partner modeling, real-time experimental systems, or robotics.

Please describe your main scientific contribution to previous work, a question you would like to pursue, and how your experience connects to our research.

<span id="graduate-students" class="lscn-anchor"></span>

## Master's & Ph.D. Students

We welcome prospective master's and Ph.D. students interested in asking precise questions about social behavior and developing experimental or computational skills. We especially welcome students with a genuine interest in neuroscience, the initiative to ask questions and pursue ideas, and the willingness to learn through uncertainty and setbacks. Research rarely follows a straight path: we value careful troubleshooting, constructive responses to unexpected results, open communication, and seeking support when needed.

In your inquiry, include your current program, expected graduation date, research or project experience, and the direction that interests you most.

**Graduate admission follows SJTU's formal application and selection procedures.** Please consult the current university and school announcements for eligibility, programs, application routes, and deadlines.

- [SJTU Graduate School — English](https://en.gs.sjtu.edu.cn/)
- [School of Biomedical Engineering](https://bme.sjtu.edu.cn/)

## Research Assistants

We welcome applicants who enjoy careful, reproducible work and want to help establish a new laboratory. Relevant experience may include animal care and training, behavioral experiments, surgery support, histology, equipment maintenance, programming, or data analysis.

Please highlight the tasks you can perform independently, the skills you want to develop, your availability, and your expected start date.

## Interns

We welcome students interested in gaining research experience in social behavior, neuroscience, or computational approaches. Please describe your interests, relevant skills, availability, and expected duration of the internship.

## How to Apply

Send your inquiry to **[mpjiang2021@163.com](mailto:mpjiang2021@163.com)**.

Use the subject line **LSCN Application — Position — Your Name**.

Please include:

1. **CV** with education, research experience, relevant skills, and publications or projects.
2. **Brief research statement** describing your experience, interests, and connection to the lab; approximately one page is sufficient.
3. **Availability:** expected graduation date, preferred start date, and intended position.
4. **Reference contacts:** postdoctoral applicants and applicants seeking Ph.D. admission after completing a master's degree should provide contact information for **three referees**. Research assistant applicants and other prospective graduate students should provide contact information for **at least one referee**. For interns, references are not required with the initial inquiry but may be requested later.

   Please include each referee's name, affiliation, relationship to you, and email address. Referees may be research supervisors, project mentors, or work supervisors familiar with your work. **Recommendation letters are not required at the initial application stage.**

Selected papers, code, or a project portfolio can help illustrate your contribution. Please describe your role in collaborative work.

## Mentoring & Lab Culture

We pursue important, exciting science and support each lab member's growth and career development. We provide ongoing mentorship, set realistic goals together, and encourage open discussion of research challenges. Through scientific discussion and opportunities to develop independence, we help members build confidence and pursue their own research questions. We value rigorous experiments, transparent analysis, thoughtful collaboration, and respectful communication.
