<p class="lscn-eyebrow">Our questions</p><h2>How does the brain make social life possible?</h2>
<p class="lscn-intro">We combine quantitative behavior, neural circuit analysis, and computational modeling to understand social behavior. Building on our work in mammalian cooperation, our research program connects five interconnected research directions.</p>
